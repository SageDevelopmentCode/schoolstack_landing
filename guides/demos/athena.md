# Athena Micro-academy of Austin landing page redesign guide

Use this guide to adapt the existing demo landing page into a personalized concept for Athena Micro-academy of Austin. The goal is not to copy their current site. The goal is to show their founder what their school could look like with a more polished, conversion-focused, emotionally resonant landing page that still feels unmistakably like Athena.

## Positioning summary

Athena Micro-academy of Austin is not positioned as a conventional Montessori school. Its current site presents it as a relationship-centered microschool for grades 6-12, launching Fall 2026, serving families looking for “a different way to do school” through personalized, self-paced learning in South Austin ([Athena homepage](https://www.amaustin.org/)).

The strongest sales angle for the redesign is:

> “Your current site has the right heart and credibility. This redesign keeps your Athena identity, but makes the parent journey clearer, more emotional, and more conversion-ready.”

## Brand translation

### Core visual direction

- **Brand feel**: Classical, academic, warm, boutique, relationship-centered.
- **Emotional tone**: Safe, small, wise, empowering, parent-relieving.
- **Visual metaphor**: Athena as wisdom, the owl as guidance, warm gold as care, navy as credibility.
- **Avoid**: Overly playful elementary-school visuals, Montessori toddler cues, generic stock education graphics, loud gradients, cartoonish icons.

### Color palette

Use a navy and antique-gold palette inspired by Athena’s existing logo and site presentation ([Athena homepage](https://www.amaustin.org/)).

| Role | Suggested color | Hex |
|---|---:|---:|
| Deep academic navy | Primary headers, nav, dark sections | `#173B5C` |
| Steel blue | Secondary headings, section labels | `#24678D` |
| Antique gold | CTAs, borders, accents | `#C1A367` |
| Sand background | Warm section background | `#F2E7D1` |
| Soft ivory | Main page background | `#FBFAF6` |
| Charcoal | Body text | `#343A40` |
| Muted gray | Captions, metadata | `#6A6F73` |

For the demo page, replace the current Sage Field greens with Athena’s navy and gold. Keep the warm natural feel, but make it more academic and middle/high-school appropriate.

### Typography

Their current site visually leans on bold uppercase headings and a classical logo treatment. For a modern redesign, use a refined pairing rather than copying the existing Montserrat/Lato look directly.

Recommended web pairing:

- **Display**: `Zodiak`, `Boska`, or `Instrument Serif` for quote moments and emotional hero lines.
- **Body/UI**: `Satoshi`, `General Sans`, or `Work Sans` for clean readability.
- **Treatment**: Use uppercase micro-labels sparingly for “Launching Fall 2026,” “Grades 6-12,” and section eyebrow text.

## Content pillars to preserve

### Parent problem

Athena’s origin story is especially strong because it starts from a real parent pain point: the founder’s son struggled socially during the transition from elementary to middle school, and a smaller environment helped improve his experience and outlook ([Our Story](https://www.amaustin.org/our-story.html)).

Use this as the emotional foundation:

- “For students who are bright, creative, and capable, but need a smaller place to belong.”
- “A school for students who thrive in close-knit spaces.”
- “A different way to do middle and high school.”

### Educational promise

The site’s philosophy centers on personalized, self-paced learning, rigorous challenge, robust support, and helping students own their learning ([Philosophy](https://www.amaustin.org/philosophy.html)).

Translate that into landing page language:

- “Personalized learning without getting lost in the crowd.”
- “Rigorous challenge, daily guidance, and space to grow at the right pace.”
- “A safe place to ask questions, make mistakes, and become more independent.”

### Program structure

Athena organizes the day around four modes: Activation Mode, Learning Mode, Skill Building Mode, and Flex Mode ([Program](https://www.amaustin.org/program.html)).

Use these as a “how the day works” section:

1. **Activation Mode**: Daily agenda, goal setting, and community building.
2. **Learning Mode**: Core academics at each student’s pace.
3. **Skill Building Mode**: Signature enrichment, including Voces and Athena’s Council.
4. **Flex Mode**: Reading, independent study, passion projects, or personal initiatives.

### Differentiators

Pull these forward as high-value sections:

- **Flex Fridays**: Academics, community service, excursions, and experiences around Austin ([Program](https://www.amaustin.org/program.html)).
- **Athena’s Council**: Life and leadership skills, including communication and listening skills ([Blog](https://www.amaustin.org/blog)).
- **Curriculum library**: Curated curriculum options designed to meet students where they are ([Curriculum](https://www.amaustin.org/curriculum.html)).
- **Year-round model**: Continuous learning with intercessions instead of the traditional August-May calendar ([Blog](https://www.amaustin.org/blog)).
- **Accredited and TEFA approved**: Use as a trust badge if confirmed by the school, since this appears in their blog announcement ([Blog](https://www.amaustin.org/blog)).

## Recommended landing page structure

### Hero

Purpose: Immediately show the school’s identity, audience, launch timing, and primary CTA.

Suggested hero copy:

> A smaller, wiser way to do middle and high school.
>
> Athena Micro-academy of Austin is a relationship-centered microschool for grades 6-12, designed for students who thrive with personalized learning, close community, and room to grow.

Hero details:

- Eyebrow: `LAUNCHING FALL 2026 · SOUTH AUSTIN · GRADES 6-12`
- Primary CTA: `Schedule a Discovery Call`
- Secondary CTA: `Explore the Program`
- Trust strip: `Accredited · TEFA Approved · KaiPod Catalyst · MSA-CESS / Vela / KaiPod affiliations if confirmed`

### Parent fit section

Purpose: Help parents self-identify quickly.

Suggested heading:

> For students who are capable, curious, and ready for something smaller.

Cards:

- **Overwhelmed by large schools**: A closer community with more room to be known.
- **Independent but still needs support**: Self-paced learning with daily coaching.
- **Socially thoughtful or sensitive**: A safer space to build confidence and belonging.
- **Ready for more ownership**: Goal setting, projects, leadership, and reflection built into the day.

### Founder story section

Purpose: Humanize the school and build trust.

Suggested heading:

> Built by an educator. Inspired by a parent’s love.

Key points:

- Mily Pérez has served K-12 students for 22 years.
- She holds an M.Ed. in Educational Leadership.
- She is a certified teacher and principal in Texas.
- She has taught and led in premier private schools in Austin and Houston, including St. Andrew’s Episcopal School and The Kinkaid School ([Meet the Founder](https://www.amaustin.org/meet-the-founder.html)).

Suggested CTA:

> Meet Mily

### Daily rhythm section

Purpose: Make the microschool model concrete.

Suggested heading:

> A day designed for focus, growth, and belonging.

Use a horizontal timeline or vertical stepper:

- **Morning activation**: Students organize, set goals, and build community.
- **Core learning**: Students progress through academics at their own pace with guidance.
- **Skill building**: Spanish language and culture through Voces, plus life and leadership through Athena’s Council.
- **Flex time**: Passion projects, independent study, reading, and personal initiatives.
- **Flex Friday**: Austin-area experiences, service, and deeper academic progress.

### Program options section

Purpose: Make tuition models easier to understand without making price the first message.

Use three cards based on their current tuition page ([Tuition/Pricing](https://www.amaustin.org/tuitionpricing.html)):

- **Full-time Program**: 5 days a week, complete student experience, annual tuition listed as $17,000.
- **Part-time 5 days**: Morning core academics, annual tuition listed as $10,200.
- **Part-time 4 days**: Afternoon enrichment and socialization, annual tuition listed as $8,426.

Note: Confirm pricing before presenting publicly.

### Why microschool section

Purpose: Explain the model to parents who are new to microschools.

Suggested heading:

> Small by design. Personal by practice.

Talking points:

- More visibility for each student.
- More flexibility than a one-size-fits-all curriculum.
- More opportunity for meaningful peer and learning-coach relationships.
- More room for independence without losing support.

### Student life section

Purpose: Make the school feel alive beyond academics.

Include:

- Student-led clubs.
- Leadership roles.
- Community service.
- Personal interests and passion projects.
- Intercession camps and after-school options.

Their student life page emphasizes clubs as a way to explore interests, build leadership, connect with peers, and support personal and professional growth ([Student Life](https://www.amaustin.org/student-life.html)).

### Admissions section

Purpose: Make the next step obvious and low-friction.

Use the current process, simplified visually ([Admissions](https://www.amaustin.org/admissions.html)):

1. Submit an inquiry.
2. Schedule a discovery call or visit.
3. Submit an application and application fee.
4. Make a deposit to secure a spot and set up a payment schedule.

CTA copy:

- Primary: `Schedule a Discovery Call`
- Secondary: `Submit an Inquiry`

### FAQ section

Suggested questions:

- Is Athena a good fit for my child?
- How is a microschool different from homeschool?
- What grades does Athena serve?
- What does self-paced learning look like?
- How does Athena support social connection?
- What is Flex Friday?
- Is Athena accredited or TEFA approved?
- What is the admissions process?

## Image direction

Use photography that feels older than elementary school and warmer than a corporate private school brochure.

Good image themes:

- Middle/high school students reading, discussing, writing, or working independently.
- Warm natural light, Austin-like outdoor or café/library settings.
- Small group collaboration around a table.
- A student looking confident, calm, or thoughtful.
- Founder portrait or founder-at-table moment if available.

Avoid:

- Preschool manipulatives.
- Overly staged classroom stock photos.
- Children who look too young for grades 6-12.
- Forest-school imagery that feels too close to Sage Field.

## Sales framing for your pitch

When presenting this to Athena, frame it as a focused admissions landing page, not a total brand replacement.

Suggested pitch language:

> I put together a quick concept showing how Athena’s existing story, colors, and admissions message could come through in a more modern landing page. I kept the navy and gold academic feel, the founder-led warmth, and the microschool positioning, but organized the page around what parents need to understand before they schedule a discovery call.

Key value points:

- Clearer parent journey.
- Stronger emotional hook.
- Better use of founder credibility.
- Easier explanation of the daily model.
- More obvious admissions CTA.
- More polished first impression for a Fall 2026 launch.

## Final quality checklist

- The hero names the school, grades, launch timing, location, and primary CTA.
- The design uses Athena’s navy and gold identity, not Sage Field green.
- The page explains “microschool” before asking parents to commit.
- Founder credibility appears within the first 3-4 scrolls.
- The daily rhythm is visual, not buried in paragraphs.
- The admissions process is simple and action-oriented.
- The page feels appropriate for grades 6-12, not early childhood.
- The final CTA repeats “Schedule a Discovery Call.”
