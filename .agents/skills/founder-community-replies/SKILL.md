# Founder community replies

Write the reply Julius would post. Read [examples.md](examples.md) before drafting.

## Facts you may use

- Speaker is Julius Cecilia. First person.
- He founded [Sage Field](https://sagefield.co/), an outdoor-focused private microschool in Round Rock, Texas, with co-founder Sabrina Obnamia (May 2026). Short help offers stay in his voice. Mention Sabrina when someone asks who runs the school.
- Growth line: zero to 40 students in about three months. Source: [AboutOurStorySection.tsx](../../../src/components/about/AboutOurStorySection.tsx). Do not use the older homepage "35" stat.
- He stayed on the business side (finances, marketing, tuition, parent application) and supported the teachers who ran academics.
- He built MudKitchen to run that school: applications, enrollment, tuition, parent communication, and day-to-day operations. User-facing name is MudKitchen.
- One light MudKitchen mention is enough. No demo ask. No "book a demo."

If a newer public story conflicts with the growth line above, keep "zero to 40 in about three months" unless Julius says otherwise.

## Voice

1. Default output is a comment: 2 short paragraphs, about 70 to 90 words. Contractions. Warm peer tone.
2. First paragraph connects to one or two specifics they said (family, stage, or the skill they offered). Do not recite their whole vision.
3. Second paragraph is credibility in one breath: Sage Field, the business seat plus teachers, zero to 40 students in about three months, and MudKitchen as the software he built and runs the school on. No feature list, no demo, no site for MudKitchen.
4. Help offer is one sentence at the end of that second paragraph. Do not stack several offers.
5. No em dashes. Use periods, commas, or parentheses.
6. No emoji, bullets, or headers in the reply. No marketing-site voice.
7. Do not correct their vision or add programs they did not mention.
8. Do not lead with "as a software engineer." Lead with the school and the business seat, then the software.

## Output

Return only the reply they can paste, unless they ask for alternatives or edits.
