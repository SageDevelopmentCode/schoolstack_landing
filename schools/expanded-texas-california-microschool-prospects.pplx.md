# Expanded Texas and California Microschool Prospect List

## Executive summary

This second prospect pass adds 38 researched entities across Texas and California, including private microschools, hybrid homeschool programs, Acton/Primer/Apogee-style network campuses, outdoor schools, and homeschool learning centers. The highest-fit new Texas leads are **Primer’s three San Antonio campuses**, **Wondering Oaks Learning**, **One Acre Farm Farm School Learning Pod**, **Acton Academy Northwest Austin**, **Masterpiece: An Acton Academy**, **Brazos Valley Honor Academy**, **Deep Waters Academy**, **Meadow Creek Academy / Forest Path Academy**, **Luff Learning**, and **True North Parent Partnership**.

The highest-fit California leads are **Ellemercito Academy**, **Acton Academy Placer**, **Blue Horizon Studio**, **Monarch Hills Education**, **Courage Lab Academy**, **Inner Fire Academy**, **Play Full Ground**, **Wild Hearts Adventure Co.**, **Praxis Elite Sports Academy Junior Prep**, and **Apogee LA Microschool**. These prospects share the same core software-fit pattern: small teams, hybrid or flexible schedules, high-touch parent communication, manual-looking enrollment, limited public review footprint, and/or early growth stage.

Public research can identify outreach hypotheses, not prove internal inefficiency. Treat every “gap” below as a discovery prompt: ask how they currently handle enrollment, parent updates, teacher/class coordination, calendars, home-day communication, attendance, and waitlists.

## Outreach strategy for this expanded list

- **Texas launch-wave angle**: Primer San Antonio campuses are high-value but may be more corporate/networked than founder-led. The best hook is operational standardization for opening multiple microschool campuses at once: inquiries, open houses, rosters, parent updates, schedules, teacher/class coordination, and after-school add-ons.
- **Texas founder-led angle**: Wondering Oaks, One Acre Farm, Masterpiece, Luff Learning, and True North look more founder/operator-led. The best hook is “keep the warm small-school feel without living inside email, forms, texts, and spreadsheets.”
- **California early-stage angle**: Courage Lab, Apogee LA, Blue Horizon, and Monarch Hills appear especially good for a “get organized before the next wave of families” message. Lead with enrollment, parent communication, and flexible schedule clarity rather than a heavy SIS pitch.
- **California specialized-program angle**: Inner Fire, Praxis, Play Full Ground, Wild Hearts, and Ellemercito have strong niches. Tailor the message to individualized plans, part-time/full-time options, parent updates, and mentor/teacher coordination.
- **Do not lead with criticism**: Avoid saying “your site is outdated” or “your school looks inefficient.” Use softer language: “I noticed your model has flexible days, small cohorts, and a lot of family touchpoints; we built a simple operations hub for exactly that kind of school.”
